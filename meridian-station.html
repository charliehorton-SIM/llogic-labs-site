The seed

Where it started.

An experiment: a civilisation of AI colonists, each with a name and an age, running their own lives on a space station. They find jobs, earn money, build new modules and raise new generations. The Founder can only take a 2% cut of their earnings and throw random events at them to see how they adapt.

What it does

Lives of their own

Personalities, careers, businesses, friendships, families and children who inherit their parents' traits.

Politics and faith

Councils, parliaments and charters, and a faith that may come to worship the Founder.

Real time

One real hour is one station day, so the colony keeps living while you're away.
